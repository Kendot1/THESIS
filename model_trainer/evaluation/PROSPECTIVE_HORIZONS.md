# Independent-horizon prospective scorer (schema 2)

`evaluation.prospective_horizons` is a read-only scorer for a **new** frozen
forecast vintage; `evaluation.prospective_freeze` seals and preflights that
vintage before its first target day. Neither retrofits the sealed October 2026
contract or activates models alone. Policy 12's multi-origin verifier is
described in `POLICY12.md`; it needs new, independently frozen and reviewed
evidence before `ModelStore.activate` can use this scorer. Policy 10 remains a
legacy 30-day pathway and does not certify these target definitions.

For an origin date, every declared category/product/variant/origin/unit series
must have exactly 19 rows: daily steps 1–14 on the next 14 calendar dates,
weekly steps 1–4 on the next four **full Monday–Sunday calendar weeks**, and
monthly step 1 on the next **full calendar month**. Weekly/monthly point
forecasts must be made independently for their period targets; this schema
does not derive them from daily rows. Match the series identity to the
publisher's canonical `category||product||variant||origin||unit` key.

The version-2 `contract.json` has these required fields:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 2,
  "target_definition": "daily14_fullweek4_fullmonth1",
  "holdout_type": "prospective",
  "model_run_id": "<exact model bundle ID>",
  "forecast_origin": "YYYY-MM-DD",
  "forecasts_frozen_at_utc": "<actual timezone-aware freeze timestamp>",
  "declared_series": ["<category||product||variant||origin||unit>"],
  "forecast_file": "forecasts.csv",
  "forecast_sha256": "<SHA-256 of exact CSV bytes>",
  "forecast_rows": 19
}
```

The minimal example above describes the scorer's target contract. For a
**preflighted** seal, `prospective_freeze` also writes SHA-256 bindings for the
candidate model bundle and metadata, selection record, forecast-input raw
snapshot and its manifest, plus the input-series count and explicitly excluded
series. Do not hand-edit these fields or backdate `forecasts_frozen_at_utc`.
`seal()` creates `contract.json` exclusively and refuses to overwrite it.

Prepare a candidate bundle finalized without activation, including its
hash-bound training source snapshot. In a fresh evidence directory, prepare:

- `forecasts.csv` with the exact 19 rows per supported series and matched
  candidate, incumbent and persistence point forecasts.
- `source_snapshot.json.gz` and `source_snapshot_manifest.json` containing the
  **raw input actually used at this forecast origin**, captured by
  `data.snapshot.capture_snapshot`. Its report dates must not exceed the origin.
- `validation.csv`, with prior chronological matched observed outcomes. It
  requires `series,origin,forecast_horizon,forecast_step,target_period_start,`
  `target_period_end,actual,prediction,champion,anchor,is_observed,`
  `observed_days,period_days`. The freeze preflight recomputes its matched
  errors, requires at least three origins **and** three distinct target
  periods for each horizon, checks exact 14/4/1 period definitions, and
  enforces at least 1/4/15 observed day(s) for daily/weekly/monthly labels.
  Optional paired `lower_80,upper_80` and `lower_95,upper_95` columns are
  checked for valid nested bounds; validation reports empirical coverage and
  mean PHP width on the supported rows. Missing bounds remain unavailable.
  Every validation target must close before selection and the new forecast
  origin. The eventual promotion verifier must still assess stable gains,
  product regressions, and genuine selection independence.
- `selection.json` with `schema_version: 2`,
  `promotion_policy_version: 12`, exact `model_run_id`,
  `model_metadata_sha256`, `forecast_origin`, timezone-aware `selected_at`,
  `declared_series`, `validation_forecasts_file`, and
  `validation_forecasts_sha256`. Optional `excluded_series` entries each need
  a canonical `series` and nonempty `reason`. Declared and excluded series
  together must exactly match the valid normalized source series.

Seal before midnight Philippine time on the first daily target date:

```powershell
$env:PYTHONPATH='D:\THESIS\model_trainer'
python -m evaluation.prospective_freeze <evidence_dir> <candidate_model_run_dir>
```

The seal uses the actual current clock; the optional clock override exists
only in Python for synthetic tests. A successful seal's digest receipt can be
rechecked with `verify_seal(contract_path, model_run_dir)` without reading
outcomes. Hashes and self-reported timestamps do not prove the forecasts were
generated by the claimed model or that future labels were never accessed;
independent replay and provenance review are still mandatory.

The `forecast_rows` example is for one series. The forecast CSV requires
`series,origin,forecast_horizon,forecast_step,target_period_start,target_period_end,prediction,champion,anchor`.
The latter three are original-PHP point prices for the candidate, incumbent,
and origin-known persistence baseline, matched on identical target keys.
Optional paired `lower_80,upper_80` and `lower_95,upper_95` columns hold
prediction bounds in PHP; leave both blank where unsupported. An interval's
nominal level is 80% or 95%, **not** a probability assigned to the point
forecast. Source forecasts and comparison predictions must be generated
causally and frozen before midnight Philippine time on the first target day.
The scorer checks the recorded timestamp and hash, but a reviewer must verify
actual file provenance, source snapshot, and selection independence.

After the **last** weekly/monthly period ends and source observations are
reconciled, create an actuals manifest with `data_file`, `sha256`,
`reconciled_through`, `source`, and `is_observed_definition`. Its CSV requires
`series,target_date,actual,is_observed`; prices use the same PHP/unit as the
forecast series. The scorer refuses to open this manifest before the final
period closes (Philippine time):

```powershell
$env:PYTHONPATH='D:\THESIS\model_trainer'
python -m evaluation.prospective_horizons <contract.json> <actuals.json>
```

One observed daily row forms a daily label. A weekly label is the arithmetic
mean of at least four valid observed dates in the **full** Monday–Sunday
period; a monthly label is the mean of at least 15 valid observed dates in the
**full** calendar month. Missing or imputed dates are never treated as
observed. Reported `observed_days` and `period_days` expose incomplete
coverage; below-threshold periods are unscored and counted by horizon. No
actual price is rounded before aggregation, while the frozen published point
may be centavo-rounded. The source observations should be audited for unusual
spikes and duplicate product/unit identities before reconciliation.

MAE and RMSE retain zero-valued observed prices; MAPE excludes actuals below
PHP 1e-8 and reports the exclusion count. Within-PHP-5, mean signed error,
candidate/incumbent/persistence matched metrics, percentage error reduction,
equal-product macro metrics, product/series/lead metrics, and 80%/95% interval
coverage and mean width are reported. An empty interval support set yields
`null` coverage, never zero. Scoring is descriptive, not a promotion verdict;
missing observations, insufficient independent origins, product regressions,
and interval undercoverage require explicit review.
